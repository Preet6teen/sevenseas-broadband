Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026. Aligned with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act and Department of Telecommunications regulations.

1. Introduction & Commitment

Sevenseas Broadband Pvt. Ltd. ("Sevenseas", "we", "us", or "our") respects the privacy of our subscribers and visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, process, and protect your personal data when you visit our website, apply for a new broadband connection, or use our residential internet services.

2. Information We Collect

To provide telecommunications services in compliance with statutory licensing requirements, we collect:

  • KYC & Identity Data: Name, government-issued photo identity (Aadhaar, Passport, Voter ID), digital photograph, and proof of residential address as mandated by the Department of Telecommunications (DoT).
  • Contact Details: Mobile number, alternate contact number, email address, and complete physical installation address.
  • Billing & Payment Data: Transaction references, plan subscription history, and payment method details. (Note: Card numbers are processed directly through RBI-authorized payment gateways and are never stored on our servers).
  • Technical & Telemetry Data: IP address allocations, MAC addresses of the Optical Network Terminal (ONT), bandwidth utilization counters, and network uptime telemetry necessary for service maintenance.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data strictly for:

  1. Verifying your identity and provisioning optical fiber connectivity at your premises.
  2. Generating monthly invoices, processing subscription renewals, and providing customer support.
  3. Monitoring network health, diagnosing Wi-Fi performance, and resolving optical line outages.
  4. Complying with statutory reporting requirements issued by the DoT, TRAI, and law enforcement agencies.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertising brokers under any circumstances.

4. Data Security & Encryption

All subscriber records and KYC submissions are stored in ISO 27001-certified data centers located within the Republic of India. Data in transit is secured using modern TLS 1.3 encryption, and subscriber records at rest are secured with AES-256 bit encryption.

5. Data Retention & Erasure

We retain customer information for the active duration of your subscription and for the statutory minimum period required under Indian telecommunications regulations. Following account termination and settlement of dues, non-statutory records are securely anonymized or destroyed.
