Self Diagnostic

Reading Your ONT Router Status LEDs

Check the small indicator lights on the front of your Sevenseas router to diagnose your line status in seconds.

PON Light: Solid Green
Optical line is healthy and synchronized with the neighborhood distribution hub.
LOS Light: Blinking Red
Loss of Signal. Check that the yellow fiber cable isn't bent, or call our NOC desk.
LAN / Wi-Fi: Blinking Green
Data packets are actively transmitting between devices and the optical network.
