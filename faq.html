1. Getting Started & Installation

If your residential society is already connected to our optical distribution ring, installation is completed within 4 to 8 business hours of your booking. For new societies undergoing riser cabling, installation takes 24 to 48 hours.

Installation is completely free (₹0) on all 3-month, 6-month, and annual plan subscriptions. For month-to-month plans, a nominal one-time cabling fee of ₹500 applies.

In accordance with Department of Telecommunications (DoT) norms, you will need: (1) Proof of Identity (Aadhaar, Passport, or Voter ID), (2) Proof of Address (Aadhaar, electricity bill, or registered rent agreement), and (3) A digital photo taken by our technician during doorstep activation.

2. Speeds & Technical Performance

Symmetrical speed means your upload speed is identical to your download speed. For example, on our Gigabit Max 500 plan, you get 500 Mbps download and 500 Mbps upload. Legacy copper cable ISPs often offer only 10% upload speed.

Fiber optics transmit data using laser light pulses through pure glass rather than electrical signals through copper. Light pulses do not degrade over long distances, generate zero heat, and are completely unaffected by heavy rain, monsoons, or electrical noise.

Because Sevenseas peers directly with local Internet Exchanges (NIXI, Extreme IX, and AMS-IX Mumbai), online gamers consistently receive sub-5ms ping to regional Indian servers in games like Valorant, BGMI, and Counter-Strike.

All Sevenseas residential plans offer truly unlimited unthrottled data. There are no daily limits, weekend slowdowns, or hidden data counters.

3. Wi-Fi Routers & Hardware

Our standard dual-band router supports up to 15 simultaneous devices. Our Wi-Fi 6 Gigabit Gateway supports 30+ simultaneous smart home devices, TVs, laptops, and consoles with zero congestion.

4. Billing, Shifting & Support

Relocation is completely free within our service coverage areas. Just notify our support team 48 hours before moving, take your ONT router along, and our field technician will reconnect you at your new address on moving day.

You can pay instantly online through UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Net Banking, Credit/Debit cards via our self-service Quick Pay portal or via direct WhatsApp payment link sent to your registered mobile number.

You can cancel at any time with zero penalty. Simply notify us via email or call our toll-free number. Our technician will collect the loaner ONT equipment, and any eligible security deposit will be refunded to your bank account within 5 to 7 business days.
